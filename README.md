## Hi there 👋

I am a fourth year PhD. student at IIT Delhi, where I work in the Laboratory for Computational Social Systems (LCS2 - https://www.lcs2.in) lead by Dr. Tanmoy Chakraborty. My primary area of research includes understanding generalization capabilities of small and efficient language models. I lead the Parmanu (Micron) group (https://parmanu.lcs2.in/) within LCS2, which explores efficient architectures, fine‑tuning, inference, model compression and scaling laws to make large language models accessible on commodity hardware.
